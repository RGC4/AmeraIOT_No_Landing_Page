# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Product pages
- Industry use case pages
- Company/about pages
- News and resources pages
- Legal pages that are publicly accessible

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboards (`/app/**`)
- Admin pages (`/admin/**`)
- API endpoints except where they affect crawlability of public pages

- Security leaders, enterprise buyers, and technical evaluators assessing Amera's cryptography and IoT security products.
